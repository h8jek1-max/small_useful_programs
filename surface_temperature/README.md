
Tas (Temperature at Surface), in the international terminology CMIP6 means an average ground air temperature of 2 meters. It is the basic building block for any climate model, because the temperature directly controls the evaporation rate of water (evapotranspiration), the onset of vegetation phases of plants and the intensity of thermal stress in biobelts.
      Climate scenario (ssp460): Previous databases (GSL and CDD) ran under the earlier scenario RCP6.0 (from the fifth IPCC report - CMIP5). This file uses the more modern equivalent of SSP4-6.0 (from the sixth IPCC report - CMIP6). 
        In terms of warming assumptions, these two scenarios are very well synchronized. Both model medium-high emissions with a total radiation effect of 6.0 W/m2 by 2100.

tas (Temperature at Surface), v mezinárodní terminologii CMIP6 znamená průměrnou přízemní teplotu vzduchu ve výšce 2 metrů.
Je to základní stavební kámen pro jakýkoliv klimatický model, protože teplota přímo řídí rychlost odparu vody (evapotranspiraci), nástup vegetačních fází rostlin i intenzitu tepelného stresu v biopásech.
